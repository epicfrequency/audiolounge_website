Auto DJ: Your Music, Rediscovered

A more effortless way to enjoy the collection you already own. New in Audio Lounge 1.1.9.

A great music collection deserves more than a search box. Auto DJ helps you rediscover albums from your own library and keeps the listening session going when your queue starts to run low.

Over time, even a carefully curated library can become too familiar. You return to the same favorite albums while the rest of your collection waits to be heard. Auto DJ makes it easier to explore what you already have—without relying on an external streaming catalog.

Rediscover Your Albums

Auto DJ offers album-based recommendations drawn from your own music library, creating more opportunities to revisit familiar favorites and find recordings you may have overlooked.

Less Queue Management, More Listening

Instead of stopping when your queued music runs out, Auto DJ can replenish the queue with more selections. Start listening, and let your collection lead you to the next album.

Built Around the Music You Own

Auto DJ is part of Audio Lounge’s library-first listening experience. Your local and NAS music collection remains at the center; you can still choose albums and manage the playback queue yourself.

Auto DJ. Your collection, rediscovered.

Auto DJ arrives in Audio Lounge 1.1.9 for Mac and Audio Lounge Air 1.1.9 for iPad and iPhone.
