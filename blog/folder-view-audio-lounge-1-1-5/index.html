Seamlessly Integrated Folder Browsing

Inspired by your feedback. New in Audio Lounge 1.1.5.

By user request, Audio Lounge 1.1.5 introduces Folder View, a new way to explore your music collection through its original folder structure.

But this isn’t just a traditional file browser. Folder View is seamlessly integrated with Audio Lounge’s existing library experience, bringing the same intelligent album organization, artwork, metadata, and playback capabilities to folder-based navigation.

Intelligent Album Recognition

Albums appear as albums, not just folders, with artwork and metadata.

SACD & Multi-Disc Integration

Stereo and multichannel SACD editions, along with multi-disc albums, retain the same intelligent grouping as Library View.

Flexible Browsing

Switch between Library and Folders, with independent Grid and List layouts.

Consistent Experience

Enjoy the same album details, favorites, filters, and playback features in either browsing mode.

Built for Large Libraries

Folder View reuses Audio Lounge’s optimized album rendering, artwork caching, and incremental loading.

Your music, your organization—without compromising the library experience.

More polish on iPhone and iPhone Duo

Audio Lounge Air 1.1.5 also continues to refine iPhone and iPhone Duo support, polishing the adaptive interface and hinge-aware Duo experience.

Audio Lounge 1.1.5 is available for Mac, and Audio Lounge Air 1.1.5 is available for iPad, iPhone and iPhone Duo.
