Reporting a bug

Please include your macOS version, Audio Lounge version, Mac model, output device, and the steps that led to the issue. Screenshots are welcome.

Sharing an idea

Feature suggestions are always welcome. A short description of how the idea would fit into your listening or library workflow is especially helpful.
