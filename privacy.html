Privacy Policy

Your music belongs to you.

Audio Lounge is built around local playback. Your library stays under your control.

Local by designYour music remains on your Mac, external drive, or network storage.
No account requiredAudio Lounge does not require a separate Audio Lounge account.
No advertising profileYour library is not used to build an advertising profile.

Effective July 24, 2026

Your library stays local

Audio Lounge reads the music files, folders, artwork, metadata, external CUE sheets, and optional PDF scores you choose to add. They remain in their original locations on your Mac, external drives, or mounted network storage. Audio Lounge does not upload your music library to an Audio Lounge cloud service.

Information stored on your Mac

Library indexes, preferences, favorites, queues, and related app data may be stored locally on your Mac so Audio Lounge can provide its features.

Purchases

Downloads and in-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Audio Lounge may check App Store entitlements to determine whether full playback has been unlocked or restored. Audio Lounge does not receive your Apple Account password or payment-card details.

App Store analytics and diagnostics

Apple may provide developers with aggregated App Store analytics, sales information, and crash or diagnostic reports according to your Apple privacy and analytics settings.

Third-party services

Audio Lounge does not require third-party advertising accounts or a third-party cloud library service. If optional online services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated before those services are used.

Deleting local data

You can remove imported folders from Audio Lounge and delete the app’s local data from your Mac. Your original music and score files are not deleted unless you remove them separately.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Audio Lounge evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
