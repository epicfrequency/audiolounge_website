# Audio Lounge Website Design Principles

- Product first. Marketing second.
- Native macOS visual language.
- Typography before animation.
- Original PNG screenshots only.
- Never convert product screenshots to JPEG or WebP.
- No stock imagery and no AI-generated UI.
- White space is part of the design.
- Technology stays behind the listening experience.
- Keep copy concise, calm, and specific.
- When unsure, simplify.
