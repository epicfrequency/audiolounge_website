How to Play SACD ISO Files on Mac and iPad

What is an SACD ISO?

An SACD ISO is a disc image: a sector-by-sector representation of a Super Audio CD. Instead of storing each song as an independent file, the image can retain the disc structure, track boundaries and stereo or multichannel areas that were present on the original release.

The audio inside an SACD is DSD rather than conventional PCM. Some discs store that stream directly; others use DST, a lossless compression method designed for DSD. Audio Lounge includes SACD ISO parsing and DST decompression: it reads the disc structure, exposes the tracks, decompresses DST when present and passes the resulting DSD into its playback engine.

What Audio Lounge handles

Audio Lounge understands the SACD filesystem, table of contents, stereo and multichannel programs, track boundaries and DSD payload. This is why adding an SACD ISO to Audio Lounge is different from opening an ISO in a general-purpose music player that only supports standalone DSF files.

SACD ISO versus extracting to DSF

Audio Lounge does not require extraction. Add the ISO itself and it becomes part of the same organized library as FLAC, ALAC, DSF and DFF. Keeping the disc as one archival object avoids creating a large folder of per-track files and preserves the original disc organization.

DSF remains useful when you specifically need one file per track, portable-player compatibility or tags embedded in each song. With Audio Lounge, conversion is an optional library choice—not a playback requirement.

Keep the ISO intact.

Audio Lounge can use the original SACD ISO as the playable library source while preserving it as the archival copy.

How to play SACD ISO on Mac with Audio Lounge

  1. Keep the ISO on the Mac, an attached external drive, or an SMB Direct share.
  2. Add that location to Audio Lounge and scan it into the library.
  3. Open the album and select a track. Audio Lounge reads the ISO and decompresses DST automatically when present.
  4. Select a local USB DAC or a HALO Endpoint directly inside Audio Lounge.
  5. Choose Native DSD, DoP or DSD-to-PCM according to the connected hardware.

Audio Lounge’s memory-buffered playback engine supports gapless transitions between compatible SACD ISO tracks. Transparent Audio Path shows the decoded source, selected processing path, output device and final rate.

For the underlying output modes, read the complete Audio Lounge guide to DSD playback on Mac.

How to play SACD ISO directly from a NAS

SACD ISO files can be several gigabytes, so a NAS is often a more practical home than the Mac’s internal storage. The NAS only needs to expose the music folder through SMB; it does not need a proprietary music-server application just for Audio Lounge.

With SMB Direct, Audio Lounge connects to the share itself. On Mac, you do not have to mount that share manually in Finder first. Add the host, share and optional subfolder in Audio Lounge, scan the location, and browse the result as part of the same library as local music.

How to play SACD ISO on iPad with Audio Lounge Air

Audio Lounge Air is the independent iPad edition, not a Mac remote. It includes the same direct SACD ISO and DST decoding path, while SMB Direct lets the iPad read a large SACD collection from the NAS without copying it into local iPad storage.

  1. Make sure the iPad and NAS are reachable on the same local network.
  2. Add the SMB share inside Audio Lounge Air; no prior Files app mount is required.
  3. Scan the location and open the SACD ISO from the organized Audio Lounge library.
  4. Select the output inside Audio Lounge Air. HALO can carry the decoded PCM, DoP or Native DSD stream to an Endpoint beside the DAC.

Network playback with HALO

HALO is Audio Lounge’s high-resolution network audio system. Audio Lounge on the Mac or iPad remains responsible for the library, queue, decoding and output decision. A lightweight Linux Endpoint beside the USB DAC receives the prepared stream.

NAS→Audio Lounge on Mac / iPad→HALO→USB DAC

HALO supports bit-perfect PCM, DoP and, with compatible endpoint hardware and DACs, Native DSD up to DSD512. The maximum useful format is determined by the source, Endpoint and DAC together. See the HALO setup guide and compatibility details.

SACD ISO FAQ

Do I need to convert SACD ISO to DSF?

No. Audio Lounge reads SACD ISO directly and presents its albums and tracks without requiring extraction to DSF first.

Can Apple Music play SACD ISO?

No. Apple Music does not treat an SACD ISO disc image as a supported music-library format. Audio Lounge has direct SACD ISO support.

Can I play SACD ISO directly from NAS?

Yes. Audio Lounge reads SACD ISO from an SMB share on Mac or iPad, so the image can remain on the NAS.

Does Audio Lounge support DST-compressed SACD ISO?

Yes. Audio Lounge decompresses DST audio from SACD ISO before playback while preserving the decoded DSD stream. For HALO playback, Audio Lounge performs the decompression before sending the DSD stream to the endpoint.

Can SACD ISO playback be gapless?

Yes. Audio Lounge uses its memory-buffered playback engine for gapless transitions between compatible SACD ISO tracks.
