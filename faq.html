Getting Started

What is Audio Lounge?

Audio Lounge is a native music player for macOS, designed for people who own and care about their music collections.

Who is Audio Lounge for?

Audio Lounge is for people who keep a personal music library and want a calm, native macOS experience for browsing and listening.

Does Audio Lounge require a subscription?

No. Audio Lounge offers a free starting experience, and a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited playback. There is no subscription.

Your Music Library

Where can my music be stored?

Audio Lounge can use music stored on your Mac, external drives, and network volumes that are already mounted in macOS.

Can Audio Lounge handle large music collections?

Yes. Audio Lounge is designed to browse, search, and manage large local libraries smoothly.

Can I use music on an external drive?

Yes. Add music from an external drive that is connected and available in macOS.

Can I use music stored on a NAS?

Yes, when the NAS share is already mounted in macOS as a network volume. Audio Lounge does not mount or configure the NAS itself.

Does Audio Lounge modify my music files?

Audio Lounge is designed to leave your music files in place. It builds its own library view around the files and locations you choose.

Playback

Which audio formats are supported?

Audio Lounge supports FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, APE, WavPack, AAC, MP3, CUE, DSF, DFF, and SACD ISO.

Does Audio Lounge support DSD?

Yes. Audio Lounge supports DSF and DFF, including DST, along with DoP output for compatible DACs and high-quality DSD-to-PCM conversion.

Does Audio Lounge support SACD ISO?

Yes. Audio Lounge can browse and play supported SACD ISO content.

Does Audio Lounge support gapless playback?

Yes. Audio Lounge supports gapless playback across compatible tracks.

Does Audio Lounge support bit-perfect playback?

Yes, on supported hardware and output configurations. Audio Lounge also supports automatic sample-rate matching.

What is the Transparent Audio Path?

The Transparent Audio Path shows the source format, decoding path, output device, and final sample rate in real time.
