Support

How can we help?

Audio Lounge is designed to feel simple. When something needs attention, the information below should help you get back to listening quickly.

Library

Keep external drives and network shares mounted before rescanning. Confirm Audio Lounge has permission to read the selected folder.

Artwork & metadata

Audio Lounge reads artwork and tags from your local files. Rescan after making changes in another tagging application.

USB DAC

Connect and power on the DAC before selecting it. For DoP, confirm that the device supports the requested DSD rate.

Purchases

Lifetime access follows the Apple Account used for purchase. Use Restore Purchases after reinstalling or moving to another Mac.

Before contacting support

  • Update Audio Lounge and macOS.
  • Reconnect external drives or USB devices.
  • Try a second album or file.
  • Note the source format, sample rate, output device, and playback path.

Contact support

Include your macOS version, Audio Lounge version, Mac model, output device, and a short description. Screenshots are welcome.
