One complete authoring workflow

No toolchain to assemble.

Conversion, DSD preparation, DST encoding, SACD authoring, and final verification belong in one continuous workflow. NativeBits brings them together in a focused Mac app—without command-line tools or a collection of specialist utilities.

Import. Master. Author. Verify.

Every step,
one Mac app.

01

Import PCM or DSD.

Bring in your source audio and organize the program for stereo, multichannel, or both.

02

Prepare the audio.

Convert PCM to DSD64, prepare DSD sources for SACD authoring, and apply DST lossless encoding.

03

Author the SACD.

Set the track sequence and add album, artist, track, and disc metadata in context.

04

Build and verify.

Generate the complete SACD ISO, reopen it, and verify the authored structure and audio data.

More than image creation

Finish with
verification.

NativeBits reopens the completed ISO and checks its authored disc structure and audio data. You finish with a result you can inspect—not an opaque file and a hope that the build worked.
